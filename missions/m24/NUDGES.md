## The approach, in plain English (open last)

1. Compute the yearly net benefit once: benefit minus running cost.
2. For NPV, start from minus the upfront cost and add each year's net benefit divided by (1 + rate) to the power of the year.
3. For payback, keep a running total starting at minus the upfront cost; in the year it would reach zero, add the fraction of that year needed. If it never does, payback is None.
4. Compute ROI with the formula given, round everything at the end, and apply the committee's rule, checking for None before comparing payback with two.
